The Game of Life

The Game of Life is one of the most exciting milestones of the InSight Program! This budgeting simulation offers a fun opportunity to imagine your life after graduation.

Need to Complete This Milestone? Schedule a Make-Up

If you missed your scheduled Game of Life session, or need to complete it on your own time, you can book a make-up appointment with a personal finance peer coach. The step-by-step guide below shows you how to reserve your spot. Use the controls to move through it at your own pace, or expand it to fullscreen.

Two Ways to Complete Your Make-Up

Once your appointment is approved, your personal finance peer coach will email you a copy of the menus, your budget sheet, and a set of instructions. From there, it is up to you how much to prepare in advance. Both paths complete the same milestone, so choose whichever fits you best.

Option 1: Come In Fresh

No pre-work needed. Show up and we will guide you.

  • Spend your appointment exploring the menus in Perry Hall.
  • Peer coaches are on hand to help you interpret your options, though you will make your budget decisions largely on your own.
  • Your coach then walks you through the Money Manager process and sends you on your way.

Plan for the full hour.

Option 2: Prep Ahead

Do some work in advance and keep your appointment short.

  • Review the instructions, budget sheet, and menus we send once your appointment is approved.
  • Complete the bulk of the budgeting work before you arrive.
  • Your coach reviews your work and runs you through the Money Manager process.

Follow the pre-work instructions and your appointment can be as short as 30 minutes.

However you choose to prepare, the sign-up process is the same. Follow the steps in the guide above to reserve your one-hour block with a peer coach.
