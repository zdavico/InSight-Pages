LinkedIn Profile Optimization

Having a strong LinkedIn profile will greatly enhance your ability to network, keep up with industry trends, stand out with prospective employers, and ultimately help you meet your professional goals.

Why LinkedIn Matters

A well-crafted LinkedIn profile serves as your professional digital presence, helping you connect with industry professionals, discover opportunities, and showcase your skills to potential employers.

Passing Requirements

You must meet the rubric requirements with a minimum grade of 80% or higher to pass this milestone. Review feedback carefully if resubmission is needed.

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1 (Optional): Drop-In Coaching Session

Visit the Career Collaborative drop-in hours @ Perry Hall (11a-4p). The Career Coach team will explain the value of an optimized LinkedIn profile and show you how to utilize it for networking based on your academic division and personal aspirations.

Step 2: Review Rubric & Polish Profile

Review the rubric and follow it section by section to polish your profile. Ensure your profile meets all requirements before submitting.

Step 3: Submit Your LinkedIn Profile

Deadline: No later than March 1st
Submit your polished profile by pasting your LinkedIn profile URL into the submission box.

Submit your profile well ahead of the deadline in order to ensure your milestone is satisfied before the deadline.

Step 4: Review Feedback & Resubmit if Needed

A Career Coach will grade your submission against the rurbic and provide feedback. If your profile scores below 80%, review the feedback and evaluated rubric to see which sections need revisions, then resubmit using the same submission box.

Students who require multiple attempts might find it productive to use the Career Coaching drop-in hours (11a-4p @ Perry Hall) to satisfy this milestone.

⚠️ Processing Time Notice

Depending on submission volume, there may be delays in receiving feedback. Please plan accordingly and submit your profile as early as possible.
