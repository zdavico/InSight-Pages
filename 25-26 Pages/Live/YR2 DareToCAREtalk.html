Join us for our annual Sarah Ramsey Strong 5k on the Burlington Bike Path, along the scenic shoreline of Lake Champlain in Burlington, Vermont. This year’s family fun run takes place Sunday, October 26, 2025 at 10 AM, starting and ending at Switchback Brewing Co., 160 Flynn Avenue, Burlington. Please plan to arrive by 9:00 AM to allow time to park, sign in and get your race bib.

The Sarah Ramsey Strong Fund is a nonprofit founded in memory of Sarah Ramsey, Champlain College Class of 2012. Race proceeds support student scholarships at Champlain College and innovation and entrepreneurship-focused student programs sponsored by the Sarah Ramsey Strong Fund.

Immediately following the 5k, celebrate with us at the race after-party for refreshments and runner awards.

Take advantage of online pre-registration for the discounted price of $30/adult ($20 for youth up to 18), and $25/student. Online registrants receive 2 free raffle tickets for the door prize drawing at the after-party. On the day of the race, walk-in registration is $35/adult and $30/college student/$20 youth (up to 18).d $30/college student/$20 youth (up to 18).

Location: Switchback Brewing Co. 160 Flynn Avenue Burlington, VT 05401 Duration: 60+ mins

A Conversation with Author J.J. Green

Join us for an insightful evening with the author of The Noise War: how to Find the Truth When Everything Is Lying to You, a field manual for a world where facts are under siege and lies travel faster than light. Author J.J. Green will be in conversation with Champlain College President, Alex Hernandez. Green is a veteran national security correspondent, podcaster, and national speaker on disinformation. The book is a clear, practical guide for journalists, policymakers, educators, and citizens determined to defend truth in an era of synthetic media and algorithmic manipulation. Free and open to the public.
