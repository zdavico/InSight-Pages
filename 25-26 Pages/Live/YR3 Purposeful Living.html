A Conversation with Author J.J. Green

Join us for an insightful evening with the author of The Noise War: how to Find the Truth When Everything Is Lying to You, a field manual for a world where facts are under siege and lies travel faster than light. Author J.J. Green will be in conversation with Champlain College President, Alex Hernandez. Green is a veteran national security correspondent, podcaster, and national speaker on disinformation.
