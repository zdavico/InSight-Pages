Resume Review & Polish

Congratulations on investing time to develop a foundational resume. A polished resume acts as an impressive introduction to employers, demonstrating your skills, achievements, attention to detail, and ability to organize information effectively.

Content & Style Matter

What information goes into your resume is just as important as how that information is written. Style, layout, font, and structure are crucial when crafting resumes that advance you to the interview phase.

Passing Requirements

Work with a Career Peer Coach to receive personalized feedback on your resume. Once you've implemented their suggestions, upload your polished resume to Handshake to ensure you have a professional document ready for employers.
