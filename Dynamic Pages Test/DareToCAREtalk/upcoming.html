Sarah Ramsey Strong 5k

Join us for our annual Sarah Ramsey Strong 5k on the Burlington Bike Path, along the scenic shoreline of Lake Champlain in Burlington, Vermont. This year’s family fun run takes place Sunday, October 25, 2026 at 10 AM, starting and ending at Switchback Brewing Co., 160 Flynn Avenue, Burlington. Please plan to arrive by 9:00 AM to allow time to park, sign in and get your race bib.

Fulfill your InSight DareToCAREtalk milestone by WALKING OR RUNNING the SARAH RAMSEY STRONG 5K RACE or VOLUNTEERING to work at the race.

The Sarah Ramsey Strong Fund is a nonprofit founded in memory of Sarah Ramsey, Champlain College Class of 2012. Race proceeds support student scholarships at Champlain College and innovation and entrepreneurship-focused student programs sponsored by the Sarah Ramsey Strong Fund.

Immediately following the 5k, celebrate with us at the race after-party for refreshments and runner awards.

Take advantage of online pre-registration for the discounted price of $30/adult ($20 for youth up to 18), and $25/student. Online registrants receive 2 free raffle tickets for the door prize drawing at the after-party. On the day of the race, walk-in registration is $35/adult and $30/college student/$20 youth (up to 18).

Students who participate through running or volunteering will need to self-report attendance

Location: Switchback Brewing Co. 160 Flynn Avenue Burlington, VT 05401 Duration: 60+ mins

Fulfills the DareToCAREtalk InSight milestone. Must participate in the run or volunteer in the event to satisfy this requirement.

Understanding Cannabis & Anxiety: For You & Your Community

Join us for an informative presentation exploring the complex relationship between cannabis use and anxiety. As cannabis becomes more widely available, understanding its mental health impacts is more important than ever. Together, we’ll learn how cannabis affects anxiety in the short- and long-term, gain knowledge to make informed decisions and recognize when you or your friends may benefit from support, and discuss available resources. This is a judgment-free space focused on education, awareness, and empowerment. All students are welcome, regardless of their personal experiences or choices around cannabis use. Facilitated by Tom Fontana (he/him), the Alcohol, Cannabis, and Other Drugs Initiatives Manager for the University of Vermont.
