How To Say Hard Things & Stay Close

Do you ever leave a conversation feeling unheard, confused, frustrated, or lonely? Communication is the heart of how we connect with others, but oftentimes no one tells us how to communicate effectively. In this workshop, you’ll learn practical, compassionate tools to handle tough talks, be understood, and stay connected. We’ll explore how family, culture, and past experiences shape our communication patterns and practice skills that help the other person truly hear you. Come with questions, scenarios, or just some curiosity!

Sarah Ramsey Strong 5k

Join us for our annual Sarah Ramsey Strong 5k on the Burlington Bike Path, along the scenic shoreline of Lake Champlain in Burlington, Vermont. This year’s family fun run takes place Sunday, October 26, 2025 at 10 AM, starting and ending at Switchback Brewing Co., 160 Flynn Avenue, Burlington. Please plan to arrive by 9:00 AM to allow time to park, sign in and get your race bib.

The Sarah Ramsey Strong Fund is a nonprofit founded in memory of Sarah Ramsey, Champlain College Class of 2012. Race proceeds support student scholarships at Champlain College and innovation and entrepreneurship-focused student programs sponsored by the Sarah Ramsey Strong Fund.

Immediately following the 5k, celebrate with us at the race after-party for refreshments and runner awards.

Take advantage of online pre-registration for the discounted price of $30/adult ($20 for youth up to 18), and $25/student. Online registrants receive 2 free raffle tickets for the door prize drawing at the after-party. On the day of the race, walk-in registration is $35/adult and $30/college student/$20 youth (up to 18).
