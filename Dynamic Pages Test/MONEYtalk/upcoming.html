Zoud GameLab

Turn financial hurdles into fun game mechanics and compete for a $140K prize pool to bring your vision to life. Zoud, the United Arab Emirates' National Financial Wellbeing and Sustainability Initiative, and Global Game Jam are teaming up for a financial literacy game jam with one of the largest prize pools in the history of game jams. We're looking for a breakout hit that makes mastering money as engaging as it is rewarding. The jam will run from Oct. 15 to Nov. 15. Not a financial literacy expert? Not a problem. Starting Sept. 15, we'll be providing resources, livestreams, access to mentors and more to help make your game idea pay off.
