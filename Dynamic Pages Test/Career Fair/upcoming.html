Vermont Tech Jam: Career + Tech Expo - October 24th

Vermont Tech Jam is an annual career and tech expo, organized by Seven Days, that showcases some of Vermont's most innovative companies. At the Vermont Tech Jam, you'll meet dozens of representatives from companies working on everything from semiconductors to software, robotics systems to battery-powered airplanes, AI to UX, and everything in between.

Since 2008, each Tech Jam event has attracted hundreds of job seekers, career changers, tech professionals, college students, enthusiasts and anyone eager to learn from and connect with Vermont companies.

Free admission. Ride the College shuttle to the Robert E. Miller Building (Champlain's Lakeside campus) and walk the short distance along Lakeside Ave to Hula.

When: Saturday, October 24th | 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM
Where: Hula, 50 Lakeside Ave, Burlington, VT

YOU MUST REGISTER AT techjamvt.com; please use your Champlain College email so that we can verify attendance.


Tickets: Free (registration required)

2027 Career Expo - February 24th

One expo, endless possibilities—your chance to explore jobs, internships, and graduate programs all in one place. This all‑majors event connects you with employers and academic programs across a wide range of industries, helping you discover opportunities that match your interests and goals.

Meet recruiters and program representatives, ask questions, and build connections that can shape your next steps—whether you’re launching your career, gaining experience, or considering graduate or professional school.

Audience: All students (any year, all majors)
Attire: Business Casual... or not*
Prep: Drop-by the Career Center the week before to brush up your resume or practice your pitch. Review the employer and grad school list on Handshake to make sure you don't miss anyone of interest...
Bonus: Employer giveaways, Career Center resources, and upcoming events

*Wearing a nicer outfit can help make a positive impression, especially if you are actively job searching - you won't go wrong with a more professional attire. That said, it's better to come as you are than not come at all - particularly if you're just scoping out options. Don't let your outfit prevent you from stopping by.
