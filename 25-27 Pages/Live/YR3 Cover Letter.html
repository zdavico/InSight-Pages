Quick Tips

  • Tailor each letter to the specific job
  • Show enthusiasm for the role
  • Highlight relevant achievements
  • Keep it concise (one page max)
Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to have a specific job in mind?

A: While it's helpful to have a target position, you can create a general cover letter template that you'll customize later for specific opportunities.

Q: Can I submit a draft for feedback first?

A: Absolutely! We encourage you to meet with your Career Coach for feedback before final submission. You can update your submission later.

Q: What if I've never written a cover letter before?

A: No problem! The submission form includes guidance for beginners, and our Career Coaches offer workshops specifically for first-time cover letter writers.
