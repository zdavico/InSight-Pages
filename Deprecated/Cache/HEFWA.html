What is HEFWA?

The Higher Education Financial Wellness Alliance is the national conference for college financial wellness professionals. Attendees include teams from Northwestern, Duke, Georgia Tech, UNC, and 50+ institutions nationwide. It is where programs like CashCourse (now used by universities across the country) were introduced and scaled.

Champlain College has been personally invited by HEFWA's conference chair to present at the July 2026 summit in Charlotte, NC.

What We're Presenting

The Game of Life (a first-year milestone for every Champlain student)  has been completely rebuilt with social constructivist learning theory as its backbone, moving students from passive financial information into active, experiential decision-making.

We'll distribute a Champlain-branded, plug-and-play resource package. All materials, training frameworks, and facilitation guides that peer institutions can implement immediately. Champlain's name will be featured on programs used at universities nationwide.

Why Peer Coaches Must Be There

  • Conducted Vermont salary research powering our financial planning models
  • Developed and tested facilitation techniques grounded in productive struggle theory
  • Operated every booth at the Game of Life event
  • Student voice is the credibility 
    Professionals don't just want to hear an administrator say it works; they want to hear it from the students who built it

How Students Benefit

  • National conference presentation: typically a graduate-level experience
  • Networking with financial wellness professionals at 50+ institutions
  • Resume credentials distinguishing them in grad school and job searches
  • Full conference attendance so peer our team can bring ideas back to enhance Champlain's programming
  • Hands-on undergraduate research experience presenting data analysis and methodology to academic audiences
