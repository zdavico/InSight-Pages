CashCourse: Post-Grad Budget Plan

The CashCourse series is a joint partnership with the Higher Education Financial Wellness Alliance (HEFWA), offering various modules aligned with the post-secondary student experience.

Step-by-Step Registration

  1. Go to CashCourse.org
  2. Click Register Now!
  3. Select "Yes, I have a code" and enter the 4-digit access code below
  4. Provide your name and email address
  5. Verify your account
  6. Pick a username (create your own or use the suggestion)
  7. Click the Let's Get Started! play button
