Available CashCourse Modules

Understanding the Basics of Budgeting - 3 Lessons | 5 Exercises | 40 min

Learn how to calculate take-home pay, balance income and spending, and begin creating a budget.

Achieving Goals - 3 Lessons | 6 Exercises | 45 min

Learn how to research and set realistic goals, utilize the SMART goal setting method, and create an action plan to achieve your goals.

Paying for College - 3 Lessons | 5 Exercises | 60 min

Learn how to find free sources to finance your higher education, analyze the various types of student aid, and determine how much student loan debt you can afford.

Creating and Maintaining a Budget - 4 Lessons | 7 Exercises | 70 min

Learn how to track income and expenses, monitor and evaluate your budget, and adjust spending habits to reduce expenses.

Understanding Credit - 3 Lessons | 5 Exercises | 34 min

Learn how to determine the cost of various types of credit based on interest and other terms, and differentiate which types of credit are better suited for particular purposes.

Obtaining Credit - 4 Lessons | 6 Exercises | 45 min

Learn how to assess the costs and benefits of various types of credit and navigate the credit application process.

Managing Credit Cards - 4 Lessons | 6 Exercises | 50 min

Learn the impacts of having and using a credit card and, if desired, how to choose a credit card to best fit your personal circumstances.

Applying for Financial Aid - 4 Lessons | 3 Exercises | 41 min

Learn how to locate and apply for financial aid from government and private sources, as well as evaluate and accept offer of financial aid.

Understanding Credit Reports and Scores - 4 Lessons | 5 Exercises | 35 min

Learn about credit scores and reports, how to create strategies to control and manage your credit, and how to dispute errors in credit reports.

Managing Student Loans While in School - 4 Lessons | 6 Exercises | 55 min

Learn how to estimate how much you will need to borrow in student loans, as well as what is affordable based on future earnings potential.

Managing Debt - 3 Lessons | 5 Exercises | 45 min

Learn how to differentiate between good and bad debt, and strategies to quickly and efficiently repay your debt.

Repaying Student Loans - 4 Lessons | 4 Exercises | 45 min

Learn how to prepare for paying back your student loans and utilize available resources and options if you experience difficulty making your payments.
