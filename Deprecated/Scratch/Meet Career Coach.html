InSight FLEX: Tailored to Your Success

The InSight FLEX program is designed to work around your individual needs, providing useful and actionable academic enhancements that align with your unique career journey. Champlain College is committed to ensuring you're career-ready by the time you walk across the stage at graduation.

One of your essential yearly milestones is meeting with your dedicated Career Coach. This isn't just another requirement—it's your opportunity to shape your future with expert guidance and personalized support.

Why This Meeting Matters

Discuss Your Goals

Share your immediate and long-term career aspirations. Your coach will help you create a roadmap to achieve them, whether you're aiming for a specific industry, role, or graduate school.

Choose Your Second Milestone

Decide which path aligns best with your needs: Career Development, Financial Literacy, or Wellbeing. Your coach can help you select the milestone that will benefit you most.

Get Tailored Opportunities

Help your coach understand your unique academic track so they can connect you with internships, job opportunities, and networking events that match your interests and skills.

What to Expect During Your Meeting

Duration & Format

Meetings typically last 30-45 minutes. Come prepared to discuss your goals, but don't worry about having everything figured out—that's what we're here to help with!

Topics We Might Cover

  • Your career interests and aspirations
  • Resume and cover letter review
  • Internship and job search strategies
  • Networking opportunities
  • Graduate school planning
  • Your second InSight FLEX milestone selection

How to Prepare

Think about your career goals, bring any questions you have, and consider bringing your resume if you'd like feedback. Most importantly, come with an open mind!
