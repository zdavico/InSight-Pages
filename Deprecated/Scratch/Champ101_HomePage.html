Welcome to Your Community! 👋

We are excited to have you as part of our cohort this semester! CHAMP 101 is designed to help you get off to a successful start at Champlain College. We know that transitions can be overwhelming, and we've specially designed this program to give you the resources you need to succeed.

Students bring a variety of life experiences, personal and social identities, strengths and challenges with them to campus, creating a diverse and vibrant community. We are committed to setting all of our students up for success from the very start!

📋 Attendance Required

You must attend 75% of your CHAMP 101 workshops to meet your First Year InSight Wellbeing milestone.

✨ No Extra Cost

Your Personal Wellbeing Matters 💪

Your personal wellbeing is a strong predictor of your success at Champlain College. As we discuss in CHAMP 101, stress in college is expected. Your path to success will be marked by inevitable challenges, including setbacks inside and outside of the classroom. We recognize that those with marginalized identities may be faced with additional stressors.

We encourage you to take an active role in taking care of yourself, and each other, throughout the semester. Please feel free to reach out to us about any difficulty that may be impacting your ability to thrive as a Champlain College student before the issue becomes unmanageable.
