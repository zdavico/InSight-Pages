Career Profile & Resume Upload

The first step on your journey to career readiness is completing your Career Profile and uploading your resume on Handshake, Champlain's online career management platform and job board.

About Handshake

Handshake is your go-to platform for discovering employment and internship opportunities throughout your academic journey and beyond. By uploading your resume, you'll help your Career Coach understand your background and create a personalized career development plan tailored to your goals.

Getting Started

This step-by-step guide will show you how to access Handshake and upload your resume.
