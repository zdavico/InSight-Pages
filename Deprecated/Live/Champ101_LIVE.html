Champ 101: First Year Wellbeing

Champ 101 is your First Year Wellbeing milestone, designed to help you get off to a successful start at Champlain College.

Why Champ 101 Matters

Transitions can be challenging. Students bring diverse life experiences, personal and social identities, strengths, and challenges to campus, creating a vibrant and dynamic community. We're committed to setting all students up for success from day one, ensuring you feel empowered to continue your education at Champlain and remain a valued member of our community for years to come.

Attendance Requirements

You must attend 75% of your Champ 101 workshops to meet your First Year InSight Wellbeing milestone.

Simply show up, connect with your peers, and engage with the knowledge and experience shared by your staff/faculty and peer facilitators.

What to Expect

  • No credit-bearing coursework
  • No out-of-classroom assignments
  • Connect with peers and learn from facilitators
  • Milestone completion confirmed in November

Additional Support

The Career Collaborative and InSight teams will visit your Champ 101 cohort to answer questions and support you in completing your Handshake milestone.
