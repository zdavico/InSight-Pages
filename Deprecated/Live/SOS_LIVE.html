SOS for Higher Ed: Suicide Prevention for Students

Champlain College is a small community where we take care of each other. It is important that we feel ready to handle situations where students may be in distress; sometimes, this means having the wisdom to know they can't handle it alone and need to seek help.

About This Training

This 30-minute, self-guided online training course uses video and interactive learning to teach you how to ACT (Acknowledge, Care, Tell) if you are worried about someone. Using real and diverse stories of mental health struggles and recovery, this training provides actionable steps to help someone in need.

Due Date

March 1st

Duration: 30 minutes
Format: Self-paced online training

What You Will Learn

1. How to recognize signs of suicide risk

2. How to have caring conversations that encourage someone to seek help

3. How to build community connections that foster positive mental wellbeing

4. An overview of available student mental health resources, on and off-campus

Training Information
