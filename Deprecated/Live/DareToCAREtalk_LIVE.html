06

Understanding Cannabis and Anxiety: For You and Your Community

Join us for an informative presentation exploring the complex relationship between cannabis use and anxiety. As cannabis becomes more widely available, understanding its mental health impacts is more important than ever. Together, we'll learn how cannabis affects anxiety—both short-term and long-term, gain knowledge to make informed decisions and recognize when you or your friends may benefit from support, and discuss available resources. This is a judgment-free space focused on education, awareness, and empowerment. All students are welcome, regardless of their personal experiences or choices around cannabis use. Facilitated by Tom Fontana (he/him), the Alcohol, Cannabis, and Other Drugs Initiatives Manager for the University of Vermont.

DACA: Dreamers and Coming to America

Artist, performer and activist Gustavo Moradel returns to Champlain for the 3rd consecutive year to tell his personal story of coming to the US at 12 years old. Gustavo touches on his lived experience as a DACA recipient, repairing his relationship with his parents, and his advocacy work in the immigration system. His story is an inspiring, educational, engaging and sobering perspective on the lived experience of over half a million DACA recipients currently living in the USA.
