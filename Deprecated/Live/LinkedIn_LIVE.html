LinkedIn Profile Optimization

Having a strong LinkedIn profile will greatly enhance your ability to network, keep up with industry trends, stand out with prospective employers, and ultimately help you meet your professional goals.

Why LinkedIn Matters

A well-crafted LinkedIn profile serves as your professional digital presence, helping you connect with industry professionals, discover opportunities, and showcase your skills to potential employers.

Passing Requirements

You must meet the rubric requirements with a minimum grade of 80% or higher to pass this milestone. Review feedback carefully if resubmission is needed.

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: LinkedIn Peer Review

Drop-Ins: Monday through Friday (11:00 AM - 4:00 PM)


The second-year LinkedIn milestone has a peer review component. You can use our drop-in hours or make a peer coach appointment (stop by the Garden House or call 802-865-5565).

Please budget around 60 minutes to complete this milestone.

Step 2: Submit LinkedIn Profile

Submit your LinkedIn URL to Canvas.

Your peer coach has grading privileges in Canvas. At the end of your coaching session, they can assign a score to your LinkedIn profile and satisfy this requirement.

Past Events

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Attend Sophomore Summit

Your Career Coach will explain the value of an optimized LinkedIn profile and show you how to utilize it for networking based on your academic division and personal aspirations.

Step 2: Review Rubric & Polish Profile

After the Summit, review the rubric and follow it section by section to polish your profile. Ensure your profile meets all requirements before submitting.

Step 3: Submit Your LinkedIn Profile

Deadline: No later than December 12th
Submit your polished profile by pasting your LinkedIn profile URL into the submission box.

Step 4: Review Feedback & Resubmit if Needed

A Career Coach will review your submission and provide feedback. If you score below 85%, review the feedback and evaluated rubric to see which sections need revisions, then resubmit using the same submission box.

⚠️ Processing Time Notice

Depending on submission volume, there may be delays in receiving feedback. Please plan accordingly and submit your profile as early as possible.
