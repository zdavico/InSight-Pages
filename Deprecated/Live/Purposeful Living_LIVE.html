Spice and Advice Series: Alex Hernandez

Think you can handle the heat? Join us for the second installment of Spice and Advice, an InSight milestone event that brings together career wisdom and increasingly spicy wings in a format inspired by the viral Hot Ones interview series. Watch President Alex Hernandez tackle progressively hotter sauces while sharing insights about his journey from teaching high school math in South Los Angeles to leading Champlain College, his experiences building career-focused education programs, and his vision for the future of higher education. As the Scoville scale rises, so does the candor. You’ll hear authentic stories, hard-won advice, and answers to your burning questions (pun intended) about navigating your career and life after Champlain. Come for the heat, stay for the wisdom. This Purposeful Living + CAREERtalk milestone offers a unique opportunity to hear directly from President Hernandez in an informal, entertaining setting where vulnerability meets a dash of humor. Students, this is your chance to watch your college president tackle the gauntlet while dropping knowledge about leadership, career journeys, and everything in between. This is an event you won’t want to miss.
