Hula Story Session: Vermont Professionals of Color Network - February 17th

Tino Rutanhira, Executive Director of Business Development and co-founder of the Vermont Professionals of Color Network (VTPoC), will share his personal journey and the story behind building an organization of support for BIPOC professionals in Vermont. Tino will highlight how minority- and diverse-owned businesses are critical drivers of our local economic development by creating jobs, generating wealth, and strengthening communities, particularly in underserved areas.

Through stories of the humans he has worked with, befriended, and supported, Tino will amplify the creativity, leadership, and resilience that professionals of color bring to Vermont every day.

4:00 PM - Doors open
4:30 PM - Welcoming remarks
4:35 PM - Feature interview
5:00 PM - Q&A
5:30 PM - Networking

The event is located at Hula, 50 Lakeside Ave, Burlington. Ride the college shuttle to the Miller Center at Lakeside and then walk the short distance to Hula.

ITScon - Spring 2026 - March 27th

Career Collaborative in partnership with the Champlain College Cybersecurity Club looks forward to welcoming recruiters from tech organizations to campus to engage in the student showcase experience and to connect with students seeking internships, part-time and full-time positions for spring semester and beyond.
ALL Champlain College students within the ITS Division are encouraged to attend and explore the wide variety of opportunities offered. Students from all majors are invited.

• Visit with recruiters from tech fields and learn about opportunities within their organizations and what you need to do to become a member of their team.
