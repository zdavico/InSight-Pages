Welcome to Champ 101!

We are excited to have you as part of our cohort this semester! Champ 101 is designed to help you get off to a successful start at Champlain College. We know that transitions can be overwhelming and we've specially designed this program to give you the resources you need to succeed, and we want you (beg you!) to take advantage of them. - Your facilitators: Pat and Steward

Students bring a variety of life experiences, personal and social identities, strengths and challenges with them to campus, creating a diverse and vibrant community. We are committed to setting all of our students up for success from the very start, so you feel empowered to continue your education at Champlain and remain a valuable member of our community for many years to come.

InSight Requirement:

Champ 101 attendance is required through the InSight Program. You must attend 75% of your Champ 101 workshops in order to meet your InSight Wellbeing milestone. This program is not credit-bearing and is included in your tuition at no extra cost. There is no out-of-classroom work.

Your Personal Wellbeing

Your personal wellbeing is a strong predictor of your success at Champlain College. Stress in college is expected. Your path to success will be marked by inevitable challenges, including setbacks inside and outside of the classroom. We recognize that those with marginalized identities may be faced with additional stressors. We encourage you to take an active role in taking care of yourself, and each other, throughout the semester.

We care about your wellbeing and can connect you to resources when you need them. We want to be a supportive presence in your college experience. Please reach out - we are here to help.

Academic Accommodations

Office of Accessibility: accessibility@champlain.edu

Accommodations are available to all currently enrolled students who have a documented disability that substantially limits them in one or more of life's major activities. Have questions about academic accommodations? Please contact the Office of Accessibility to schedule a meeting.

Academic Coaching & Peer Tutoring

  • Academic Coaching - Connect with an academic coach for 1:1 support to help with completing assignments on time, task initiation, and learning strategies for success in the classroom. Sign up for an Academic Coaching appointment—it's easy!
  • Peer Tutoring Centers - Peer Tutoring is available for many of your classes and a good way to get support from a fellow student. Check the schedule here.

Student Success Advisor
