Resume Review & Polish

Congratulations on investing time to develop a foundational resume. A polished resume acts as an impressive introduction to employers, demonstrating your skills, achievements, attention to detail, and ability to organize information effectively.

Content & Style Matter

What information goes into your resume is just as important as how that information is written. Style, layout, font, and structure are crucial when crafting resumes that advance you to the interview phase.

Passing Requirements

You must meet the rubric requirements with a minimum grade of 85% or higher to pass this milestone. If your initial submission doesn't meet this threshold, you'll have the opportunity to revise and resubmit.

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Attend Sophomore Summit

Date: Wednesday, September 15th
During Sophomore Summit, you'll meet your Career Coach and attend a working session to polish your resume. This event is central to all your sophomore year milestones.

Step 2: Revise Using the Rubric

Access the rubric here and make necessary changes to ensure your resume meets every requirement before submitting.

Step 3: Submit for Review

Deadline: No later than October 1st
File naming: FirstNameLastNameResume.doc (or .docx)
Submit using "Start Assignment" and confirm you see "Turned In" status.

Step 4: Review Feedback & Resubmit if Needed

Career Collaborative staff will provide feedback directly in Canvas. If you score below 85%, revise and resubmit with the filename: FirstNameLastNameResume2.doc (or .docx).

⚠️ Processing Time Notice

Depending on submission volume, there may be delays in receiving feedback. Please plan accordingly and submit your resume as early as possible.
