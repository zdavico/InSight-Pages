The Game of Life

The Game of Life is one of the most exciting milestones of the InSight Program! This budgeting simulation offers a fun opportunity to imagine your life after graduation.

Event Details & Attendance

When: October 1st or 2nd (mirrors your your Champ 101 schedule)
Where: Meet in your regular Champ 101 classroom, then walk together to the Champlain Room
Format: Cohort-based participation (you may be paired with another cohort)

Can't attend? If you're unable to participate on your scheduled date, please contact Team InSight | insight@champlain.edu to arrange alternative materials.

Accessibility Information

We're committed to making this event accessible for all participants:

  • Space: Up to 50 people may be present; occasional crowding is possible
  • Audio: Low background noise only. No loud sounds or flashing lights
  • Interaction: One-on-one conversations with staff, faculty, and student employees
  • Math Support: Calculators provided and budget calculation assistance available

If you have specific accessibility needs or concerns, please reach out in advance so we can ensure your full participation.
